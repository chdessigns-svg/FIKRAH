Eng. Nassor Morsady Hassan

Eng. Nassor Morsady Hassan

Chairman, Fikrah Summit
The Power of Presentation

Chairman & Electrical Engineer

Eng. Nassor Morsady Hassan chairs Fikrah Summit, steering the community's weekly sessions and long-term direction since its evolution from DOUBLE G. As an electrical engineer, entrepreneur and business leader, he brings a practical, builder's perspective to Fikrah's focus on leadership and presentation skills.

His session, "The Power of Presentation," is one of Fikrah's most referenced talks — making the case that technical competence alone rarely moves a room; how an idea is presented is often what decides whether it gets acted on.

Beyond chairing sessions, he is closely involved in guiding Fikrah's growing membership base and setting the standard for the discipline, punctuality and preparation the community expects of every speaker who takes the floor.
