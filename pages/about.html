Our Story

Built by people who believe in showing up weekly.

Fikrah Summit is a professional networking and personal development community bringing together entrepreneurs, professionals, executives, students and future leaders to exchange knowledge, build meaningful relationships and inspire personal and business growth.

Originally founded as DOUBLE G (Givers Gain), the organization evolved into Fikrah Summit with a renewed philosophy and stronger focus on leadership, entrepreneurship, business excellence and lifelong learning. What began as an informal gathering of like-minded professionals in Dar es Salaam has grown into a structured platform hosting over 100 sessions, drawing CEOs, academics, engineers and students into the same room every week.

Sessions have moved from the community's earlier home at the An-Nahl Offices to its current venue at the Adil Conference Center, reflecting the community's steady growth in both size and ambition.

The journey continues. Weekly learning • Guest speakers • Networking • Mentorship

Core Values

Principles that shape us.

Fikrah is built around a simple belief: excellence grows when people learn, serve, collaborate and lead with integrity.

01

Leadership

Every member is encouraged to lead — in their business, their community, and inside every Fikrah session.

02

Integrity

We speak honestly, keep our commitments and hold each other accountable to the highest professional standards.

03

Knowledge

Weekly sessions exist to transfer real, practical knowledge from experienced leaders to the next generation.

04

Innovation

We welcome new ideas, new industries and new ways of solving old problems in business and society.

05

Collaboration

Growth is faster together. Fikrah exists to connect people who can build with, not just around, each other.

06

Service

True leadership starts with service — to members, to clients, and to the wider Dar es Salaam community.

07

Growth

Personal and business growth is never finished. Every session is a small step toward a bigger version of ourselves.

08

Excellence

We hold our sessions, our speakers and our own conduct to a standard worth returning for, week after week.
