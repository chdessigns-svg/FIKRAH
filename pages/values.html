Core Values

Principles that shape us.

Fikrah is built around a simple belief: excellence grows when people learn, serve, collaborate and lead with integrity.

01

Leadership

Every member is encouraged to lead — in their business, their community, and inside every Fikrah session.

02

Integrity

We speak honestly, keep our commitments and hold each other accountable to the highest professional standards.

03

Knowledge

Weekly sessions exist to transfer real, practical knowledge from experienced leaders to the next generation.

04

Innovation

We welcome new ideas, new industries and new ways of solving old problems in business and society.

05

Collaboration

Growth is faster together. Fikrah exists to connect people who can build with, not just around, each other.

06

Service

True leadership starts with service — to members, to clients, and to the wider Dar es Salaam community.

07

Growth

Personal and business growth is never finished. Every session is a small step toward a bigger version of ourselves.

08

Excellence

We hold our sessions, our speakers and our own conduct to a standard worth returning for, week after week.
